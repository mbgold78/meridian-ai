# Task 1 — Read the portfolio dashboard: what needs you today

What this task teaches: the five zones of the corporate dashboard, which of
them are live engine data, and the reading order that surfaces what needs
your attention first.

## Entering the portfolio

1. Go to **`/portfolio`**. Two vantage points are offered:

{{capture:portfolio-landing}}

   - **Corporate Operating Partner** — the federated view across every
     subsidiary. This task uses it.
   - **Subsidiary COO** — one operating company's view (task 4 enters it).

2. Click **Enter portfolio dashboard**.

## Read the dashboard

3. Read top to bottom — the page is ordered headline → detail:

{{capture:portfolio-dashboard}}

   - **Header strip** — portfolio identity figures, plus a provenance chip
     on the right stating which parts of the page are live. Hover it for the
     precise breakdown.
   - **Coordination signals** — four cards. *Agents in intervention* and
     *Lifecycle stalls* and *Coordination signals* are **live engine
     answers**; each card carries its own provenance note. When a registry
     is unreachable a card shows an em-dash and says counts are withheld —
     it never fabricates a zero.
   - **Where each subsidiary sits** — the phase grid. Illustrative data;
     the *Lifecycle stalls* card above is the honest live count, and when
     the two disagree, trust the card.
   - **Where coordination is failing** — opportunity cards. Cards promoted
     to a documented coordination contract are clickable (task 3 follows
     one).
   - **Cascades you've issued** — **live**. Cards order themselves by
     urgency: **Decline pending** (red — a level below has formally pushed
     back; this needs the issuer) first, then overdue, then the rest. Only
     in-flight cascades appear; the section disappears when nothing is in
     flight.

4. The reading habit this page is built for: **scan the four signal cards,
   then the cascade chips, before anything else.** Red chips are queues of
   decisions waiting on a leader — task 2 opens one.

## You'll know you've read it right when…

…you can answer, without scrolling back: *how many agents need human
intervention right now, which cascade is waiting on its issuer, and which
numbers on this page are live versus illustrative* — and the answer to the
last one came from the provenance notes, not from guessing.
